Seven years after the Lycians commit deicide, Sarmania demonstrates the same
capability. In a desperate act, the Sarmanians deploy the full capabilities of
Ganzir in battle and kill Min.

The war ends, but Na-Keb does not forget. Tensions remain along the border as
Na-Keb continues to lose land to Sarmania.

**Dating note:** 18 years ago is derived from Godsfall being 25 years ago and
Min’s death occurring seven years later. No absolute calendar year is established.
