Lycia rebuilds its identity. The Apolitain League balances divine government
against demands for change. Maurkim’s trading houses compete for influence,
Na-Keb pursues its great projects, and Sarmania presses its conquests.

Travel away from civilization is dangerous, and monsters roam the countryside.
The world exists beyond the adventurers—but their actions can change it forever.
