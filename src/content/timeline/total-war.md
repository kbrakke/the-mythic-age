Sarmania attempts to annex ancient Lycian colonies on the northern shore.
The People’s council of Lycia responds with an overwhelming **846–154** vote
to enter a state of total war.

According to the world overview, the war has continued ever since. The colonies
are both a practical source of food and a symbol of Lycia’s struggle against
divine rule.
