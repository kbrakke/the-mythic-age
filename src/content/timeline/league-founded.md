Even-Handed Alexandria and her son, wizened Timo, are remembered as the first
of Polita’s champions to unite the cities, creating the Apolitain League as it
exists today.

The League’s stories of this age describe gods and heroes working in tandem:
divine champions embodied their patrons’ ideals and helped shape civic life.

**Chronology note:** the world documentation does not give a year for this event.
