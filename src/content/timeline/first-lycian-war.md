The early years following Godsfall include a short war against the Apolitain
League. The account of Lycia describes the fighting as closer to a skirmish than
a prolonged campaign.

At home, the new order also undertakes a long and bloody effort to root out
Pontinate sympathies. Surviving forces loyal to the old empire continue to resist.

**Chronology note:** this event is placed near Godsfall for reading order. Its
exact year, and its relationship to the other dated events, are not established.
