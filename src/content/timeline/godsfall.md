Godsfall devastates the northern territories, leaving ruins and damaged
infrastructure throughout the countryside. The collapse of the Pontinate
pantheon opens a power vacuum, filled by revolutionary ideas of self-governance
and human autonomy.

From the former heart of the Pontinate Empire emerges a passionate democracy.
Citizens vote directly on questions ranging from infrastructure to military
campaigns. Beyond its borders, deicide forces mortals and gods alike to confront
what Lycia has done.
