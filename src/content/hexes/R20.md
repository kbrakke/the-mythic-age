---
title: The Death of Min
terrain: desert
tags: [history, sarmania, na-keb]
---

Seven years after Godsfall, Ereshkigal brought the sky citadel Ganzir here and slew Min,
God of the Desert, ending the war between Sarmania and Na-Keb in a single act.

Na-Keb has never forgotten the crime. Its hold on the surrounding desert has weakened ever
since, and the border hexes to the north continue to slip toward Sarmania.

See [Sarmania](/world/sarmania/) and [Na-Keb](/world/na-keb/).
