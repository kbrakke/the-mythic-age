The place where the Pontinate pantheon died, twenty-five years ago. The countryside for
miles around is still scarred with ruins and crumbling infrastructure from the collapse.

Lycians treat the site with a mix of pride and unease. The bands of Gaius Marius Maximus,
remnants of the old imperial legion, are known to move through the hills north of here.

See [Lycia](/world/lycia/) for the wider history.
